## Fulfillment Workflow (Draft v1)

```
[CLIENT INTAKE]
   ↓ Order received on Fiverr
   ↓ Auto-reply with intake form (Google Forms / Typeform)
   ↓ Collect: topic, keywords, word count, tone, examples, deadline

[BRIEF & ALIGNMENT]
   ↓ Review intake within 2 hours
   ↓ Confirm requirements or ask clarifying questions
   ↓ Set internal deadline (buffer before Fiverr deadline)

[PRODUCTION]
   ↓ Outline → Client approval (optional, for large orders)
   ↓ Draft 1 → Submit via Fiverr
   ↓ Internal QA (Justina spot-check on first few)

[REVISION CYCLE]
   ↓ Client feedback (48hr window)
   ↓ Revise → Submit Draft 2
   ↓ (If needed) Final polish → Submit Final
   ↓ **Policy: 2 revision rounds included, thereafter chargeable**

[DELIVERY & CLOSE]
   ↓ Final file + source doc
   ↓ Request review
   ↓ Add to portfolio (with permission)
   ↓ Mark complete
```

**SLAs:**
- Intake response: 2 hours
- First draft: Per gig tier (Basic/Standard/Premium turnaround)
- Revision turnaround: 24 hours
- Communication: Daily during active orders
